AI Tools Guide · 1 of 4

Chat Assistants

Conversational AI you can talk to in plain language. Use them to draft, explain and brainstorm, then check the result yourself.

C

ChatGPT

by OpenAI

What it is

A general-purpose chat assistant that answers questions, explains ideas and drafts text.

Good for

  • Explaining difficult concepts step by step
  • First drafts and outlines
  • Practice questions for revision
Watch outIt can sound sure of itself while being wrong. Check facts against your textbook or notes.
Visit chatgpt.com ↗
C

Claude

by Anthropic

What it is

An AI assistant that is strong at reading long documents and writing carefully.

Good for

  • Summarising long readings or reports
  • Editing and improving your own writing
  • Talking through a problem in steps
Watch outSummaries can miss details. Read the original for anything you will be marked on.
Visit claude.ai ↗
G

Gemini

by Google

What it is

Google’s AI assistant, which works alongside services such as Search, Docs and Gmail.

Good for

  • Quick help if you already use Google apps
  • Brainstorming project ideas
  • Summarising text you paste in
Watch outDo not paste private or confidential details into any chat assistant.
Visit gemini.google.com ↗
